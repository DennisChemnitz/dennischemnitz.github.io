Random dynamical systems · Stochastic analysis

Dennis Chemnitz

Postdoctoral researcher
Department of Mathematics · ETH Zürich

I study the dynamics of stochastic evolution equation. I am particularly interested in system arising in stochastic fluid dynamics and the theory of machine learning.

I am funded by DFG SPP 2410 “CoScaRa”. I am also an external member of the Stochastic Analysis in the Sciences (SAiS) research group, led by Benjamin Gess.
